Some of the finest pianos ever built are decades old, and can be acquired
for a fraction of their modern equivalents. But with older instruments,
condition is everything.

## What actually holds value

Marque and model matter, but so do the soundboard, pinblock and the
quality of any restoration. A famous name over a tired pinblock is not a
bargain.

## The independent inspection

Before you commit, an independent technician should examine structure,
soundboard and action, and forecast any restoration cost. That single
step turns hope into certainty.
