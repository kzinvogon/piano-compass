The Feurich OLYA EVO brings the Feurich name to a compact digital piano: 88 weighted, hammer-action keys, three pedals, headphone output for quiet practice, and MIDI, USB and line in/out connections for apps and recording. It comes in black or white. For a first instrument in a small room it is excellent value — and as an independent advisory we will tell you honestly when an acoustic upright would serve you better. Book a consultation to compare it against the alternatives for your playing, space and budget.
